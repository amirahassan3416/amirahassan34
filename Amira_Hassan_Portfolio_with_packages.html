Medical AI with Retrieval-Augmented Generation

AI Hackathons · CREATIVA Hub, Ismailia · Team Synaptix, Top 5

Best Participant award

Purpose

Our team built a medical assistant that looks up the most relevant passages in trusted medical guidelines first, then uses them as context to write the answer. This keeps responses relevant and reduces answers that the sources don't support.

Implementation

The interface is a Gradio app called WHO Hypertension AI Assistant. It answers from the WHO Guideline for the Pharmacological Treatment of Hypertension in Adults (2021), marks each answer as verified or low evidence, and shows the retrieved passages with their page range, chunk number and a match-confidence gauge.

  • RAG
  • Embeddings
  • FAISS
  • Vector search
  • Evaluation
  • Python
  • Gradio

Results

  • Selected among the Top 5 teams at the AI Hackathons, CREATIVA Hub, Ismailia.
  • Awarded Best Participant, with 40 hours of practical AI solution development.
  • Measured retrieval quality and response quality, to check that the passages we retrieved and the answers we generated were good.
  • Improved relevance and reduced unsupported answers by grounding responses in the guideline.
